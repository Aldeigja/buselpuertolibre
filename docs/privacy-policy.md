# Privacy policy — Horarios Bus El Puerto

Last updated: November 2026

Horarios Bus El Puerto (`es.elpuertobus.app`) is an independent offline bus timetable and map for El Puerto de Santa María. It is not an official government service.

## Summary

The app does not use accounts, analytics, or advertising. It does not send your timetable lookups, stop choices, or map tiles to a server operated by this app. Bundled timetables, the map snapshot, and holiday dates are stored in the app package.

## Location

If you tap **My location**, the app may ask for device location permission. Location is used only to show your position on the map on this phone. It is not transmitted to us or to third parties. You can decline permission and still use the map and timetables.

## Data stored on your device

- **Language choice:** If you switch between English and Spanish, that preference is stored on the device.
- **Android backup:** If you enable device backup, Android may include app preferences (such as the language choice) in the backup. The app does not store a history of your location.

## Data we do not collect

- No account or sign-in
- No analytics or crash reporting SDKs in this release
- No sale of personal data
- No contacts, photos, or messages

## Third-party data in the app

- Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Attribution is shown in the app.
- Public holiday dates from the Junta de Andalucía work calendar (CC BY 4.0), bundled in the app.
- Bus timetables are compiled from public sources named in the in-app About screen. Timetable data is not licensed under Apache-2.0.

## Children

The app is not directed at children.
